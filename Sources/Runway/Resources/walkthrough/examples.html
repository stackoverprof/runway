/walkthrough: templates, use cases, scenarios

Every slide is a 2184x2184 PNG: eyebrow, headline, evidence, 2 or 3 findings, provenance footer. Pick one layout for the evidence and one theme for the surface. Every example below is a real exported slide; click to open full size. Kept in the skill as examples.html; open it when choosing a layout.

1. Layouts: how the evidence sits on the square

Chosen by the shape of the screenshot.

1b. Marks: point at the exact spot

Thin ring in a color picked to contrast with the screenshot (never the brand red on a red header), white edge, soft halo. Numbered when there are two or three, and the finding starts with the same number. No arrows, no dimming.

2. Themes: which surface the shot is from

Set per slide with data-theme. One deck often mixes them (bug on the storefront, guard in the dashboard). The surface in the screenshot decides, not the issue's tag.

3. Provenance footer: always true
